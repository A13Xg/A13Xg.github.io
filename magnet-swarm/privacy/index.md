**Effective date:** November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **A13Xg Industries** ("we", "us") handles information in the **Magnet Swarm** mobile game (the "App") for iPhone and iPad.

## Summary

- We do **not** require an account, and we do **not** ask for your name, email, or contacts.
- Your game progress is stored **only on your device**.
- We do **not** operate our own servers that receive data from the App.
- The App shows ads using **Google AdMob**, which may collect device and usage information as described below.
- Purchases are processed entirely by **Apple**. We never see your payment details.

## Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress, unlocked upgrades, settings, and purchase entitlements (for example, whether you bought "Remove Ads") in local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect:

- Device identifiers, including the **Advertising Identifier (IDFA)**, *only if you allow tracking*
- IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (coarse) location
- Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)
- Ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completions)
- Diagnostic and performance data related to ads

**App Tracking Transparency:** On iOS 14.5 and later, the App asks for your permission before allowing Google to access your device's Advertising Identifier. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the IDFA is not shared and you will see non-personalized or less-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising controls](https://adssettings.google.com)

Buying **Remove Ads** stops banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch them.

## In-App Purchases

In-App Purchases (such as Aether Shard packs and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive only a confirmation that a purchase occurred, so the App can unlock the item. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

## Your rights and choices

- **Limit ad tracking:** Deny or revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings as described above.
- **Delete your data:** Uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data. We hold no copy of your game data.
- **Google data:** Requests about data collected by Google AdMob can be made through Google's privacy tools linked above.
- Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of personal information. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most such requests are handled by Google for ad data. You may still contact us and we will assist.

We do not sell your personal information. Sharing of device data with Google for advertising, where you have permitted tracking, may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out at any time by denying tracking permission.

## Data retention

We do not retain any data, because we don't receive any. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

## Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by iOS security features. Communication between the App and Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
