## Frequently asked questions

**How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?**
Open the in-game shop and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

**I bought Aether Shards but didn't receive them.**
Fully close and reopen the App while connected to the internet. If they still don't appear, email us with the approximate purchase time. For refunds, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Will I lose my progress if I delete the App?**
Yes. Progress and unspent shards are stored only on your device. "Remove Ads" can be restored, but consumable shards cannot.

**Why am I asked about tracking?**
Apple requires apps to ask before ad partners can use your device's advertising identifier. The game works the same either way. See our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details.

**How do I watch an ad for free shards?**
Rewarded ads are optional and can be watched up to 3 times per day from the shop for +15 Aether Shards.
